About the System

The Smart Campus Issue & Resource Management System provides a centralized platform for students, faculty, and administrators to report, track, and manage campus-related issues and resources.

Key Features

📝

Report Issues

Students and faculty can report problems related to classrooms, infrastructure, facilities, and campus services.

📊

Track Issues

Monitor the status of reported issues from submission to resolution.

👥

Role-Based Access

Separate portal views for students, faculty, and administrators.

📦

Resource Management

Search and view information about available campus facilities.
